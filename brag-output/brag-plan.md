# Brag Plan: Q-Trust

## What is this app?

Q-Trust is a post-quantum cryptography migration and attestation protocol: it scans a cryptographic estate, ranks what needs to move first, and anchors every milestone as a public, wallet-free record on Base L2.

## The angle

RSA has a retirement date. Most migration programs have a spreadsheet and a promise.

The video starts with the public NIST deprecation window, not a generic quantum warning, then follows the proof: the exact Q-Trust promise, a working scan, the ranked exposure, and a verified record. The last scene leaves the dark product surface for the site's editorial light canvas, so the record literally leaves the room.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

A dark grid. `NIST IR 8547` appears like a system label, then `2030â€”2035` slams into the frame at display scale. Under it: `RSA / ECC signatures disallowed.` A sample exposure ledger ticks in on the right. The hook is the deadline itself.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **The scan resolves.** The cursor presses `Run scan`; the progress rail fills; `284` assets, `39` at risk, and `68%` PQC ready count up inside the workspace.
- **The estate is ranked.** Three sample assets line up with real-looking scores: `api-gateway / tls` / `RSA-2048` / `87` / `Migrate`; `payments / signing` / `ECDSA P-256` / `74` / `Prioritize`; `mobile / key exchange` / `ML-KEM-768` / `12` / `Ready`.
- **The record becomes public.** A hashed migration record is checked on Base Sepolia with no wallet. The status flips to `Attestation verified`, then a seal pulse lands on the music.

## Outro / punchline

The frame turns to warm editorial paper. `Evidence, not promises.` lands in dark ink and Q-Trust teal. The final line is the product's own CTA: `Start with what you can prove.` The mark, Base L2 / hash-only / open-verification badges, and the live URL close the loop.

## User flow worth showing

1. **Entry:** scan the estate.
2. **Key action:** rank the exposure and settle the record.
3. **Result:** a public, wallet-free on-chain verification.

The centerpiece is the working-workspace recreation, not a section list.
